Features

Chain view

Chain Superposition Engine

Stack without the guesswork. Copy State on each TDR Nova in your channel and paste it here. One Curve draws every layer and shows you the yellow summed response so you instantly spot conflicting boosts or harsh resonant buildups.

Match view

Match & Dial Engine

Turn that chaotic stack back into fewer bands. Match the target curve, copy the state with one click, and paste it back into your DAW. Built for TDR Nova and Nova GE today.

Blindly Fighting Ghost Frequencies

When you keep chaining EQ after EQ to patch up problems created by earlier plugins, you lose track of what is actually happening to your sound.

  • Toggling between open plugin windows to figure out where mud came from
  • Accidentally boosting frequencies that an earlier EQ cut
  • Band-aiding mix problems downstream rather than fixing them at the source
Outcome: Muddy sound, ear fatigue, and lost mix decisions
Single-Plugin Clarity 1 Clean Curve

One Clean Single-Plugin Answer

See the net acoustic reality in seconds. Recreate that exact shape with a focused TDR Nova right at the start of your chain.

  • Total visual clarity on your channel's actual frequency curve
  • One single plugin window to tweak whenever your mix needs adjusting
  • Confidence that you're shaping music, not wrestling plugin clutter
Outcome: Punchy, transparent, and effortlessly controllable audio
